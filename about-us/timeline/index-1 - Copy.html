The garage phase

The idea of an electric supercar was born.

Rimac Automobili is Born

The prototype Concept_One was first shown at the Frankfurt Motor Show.

Getting Traction

Delivery of the first car developed and manufactured for another automotive company - a Concept_One prototype went global as the FIA Formula E Race Director's car.

Engineering Excellence

The C_Two, today known as the Nevera, was launched at the Geneva Motor Show with performance and character that elevated the genre.

Production Ready

The production version of the Nevera was launched; Rimac Automobili and Bugatti merged into Bugatti Rimac, the best hypercar company in the world.
